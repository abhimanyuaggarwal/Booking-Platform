# Samvad demo film, Hindi narration

The film `docs/demo/samvad-demo-hindi.mp4` (3 min 22 s) tells the same story as the deck, beat for beat:
the live, the by-hand mess, the four pieces, Meera's evening, the team's week, the settings, the phone-and-cash
booking, Hindi, the promise. The voice on it today is a computer voice (macOS "Lekha"), which is the one part
that still sounds made by a machine. A person reading the same lines makes it sound made by people.

## Recording a human voice

1. A quiet room, a phone's voice memo app held about 20 cm from the mouth, or a laptop with any USB mic.
2. Read one beat per recording, at an easy pace, with a half-second of silence before and after. Re-record a beat if
   it stumbles; do not try to read the whole script in one go.
3. Name the files by beat id: `00-cover.m4a`, `01-scene.m4a`, … `13-ask.m4a` (m4a, wav or mp3), and put them in one folder.
4. Send the folder to Abhimanyu. Rebuilding the film with those files in place of the computer voice takes a few
   minutes (`docs/demo/film/assemble.py` with `VOICE_DIR` pointing at the folder); every beat stretches or holds its
   last frame to fit the voice, so timing needs no editing.

## How the film is built (for whoever rebuilds it)

- `cards.html` + `assets.mjs`: the story cards, rendered to PNG with Playwright, in the deck's visual language.
- `record-journey.mjs`: records her phone (WhatsApp, pay, booking page, waiting room, video room) and guruji's phone
  against the live pilot server; needs the console login, the guru token, and one paid booking about to start.
- `record-desk.mjs`: records the console's Week calendar and Settings.
- `narration.json`: the lines above; `say -v Lekha -r 168` makes one AIFF per beat when no human recording exists.
- `assemble.py`: cuts each beat to its voice, overlays the caption, and joins everything with ffmpeg.
